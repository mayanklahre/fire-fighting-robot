## Evidence claims and limits

The images substantiate that a physical wheeled prototype existed. They do not independently prove:

- the exact Arduino board, sensor models, L298N connections, pin assignments, voltage ratings, or firmware version;
- flame-detection, obstacle-avoidance, temperature-check, motor, servo, or pump performance;
- detection range, accuracy, extinguishing time, or safety certification.

Those details remain intentionally marked as pending until the original Arduino sketch, verified pin map, and controlled test evidence are added. The prototype should be treated as a controlled educational build, not real fire-safety equipment.
